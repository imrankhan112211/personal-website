This page is a **layout example**. Your real articles will look like this.

## Start with the answer

Readers and AI search tools both prefer articles that answer the main question in the first few lines, then explain.

## Use clear headings

Each `##` heading appears in the table of contents on the left.

- Short paragraphs
- Practical steps
- Links to sources

> One strong sentence can be highlighted as a quote.

## Link to your services

When it fits, link to the [consulting page](/consulting/) or [training page](/training/).
